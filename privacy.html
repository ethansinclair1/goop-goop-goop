Goop Goop Goop

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Goop Goop Goop is a small Discord Activity. It shows a picture you choose on a map background with speech bubbles about Civilization VI. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Goop Goop Goop has no accounts, no server and no database. It doesn't ask Discord for your username, profile, friends list or messages, and it doesn't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

The picture you choose

When you pick a GIF or image, it stays on your device. It's saved in your browser's local storage so it's still there next time you open the Activity on the same device. It's never uploaded to us or shared with other players. You can remove it by clearing site data for the Activity in your browser or Discord app.

Discord

The Activity runs inside Discord, so Discord's own Privacy Policy covers your use of Discord itself. When the Activity opens it sends Discord a standard startup message so Discord can show it. That message contains the app's ID, not information about you.

Hosting and fonts

The Activity's page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services may receive basic technical details such as your IP address when the page loads. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

Children

Goop Goop Goop is meant for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's own age rules. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer through Discord.
